# Platform Canvas — Module 9 Group Exercise (25 min)

Work in groups of 3–4. You are designing (or redesigning) an **internal
developer platform** for an organisation one group member knows well.
Fill in every box — bullet points, not essays. Present in 2 minutes.

> A platform is a **product**: it has users, competes with alternatives
> ("I'll just do it myself"), and lives or dies by adoption.

---

## 1. Users & Personas

*Who consumes the platform? (e.g. backend teams, data engineers, QA)*

-

## 2. Jobs to Be Done

*What do users need to accomplish, in their words? ("Deploy a new service to
production without filing a ticket")*

-

## 3. Pain Points Today

*What is slow, risky, or toil right now? What do people complain about?*

-

## 4. Golden Path Offering

*The paved road: what does the platform provide end-to-end for the most
common use case? (template repo → CI/CD → runtime → observability)*

-

## 5. Self-Service Interface

*How do users consume it — CLI, portal, Git template, service catalog?
What is the time-to-first-deploy for a brand-new team?*

-

## 6. Guardrails, Not Gates

*Which policies are enforced automatically (security scanning, resource
limits, naming), so humans do not need to review routine changes?*

-

## 7. Platform SLOs & Support Model

*What reliability does the platform promise its users? Office hours?
On-call? Escalation path?*

-

## 8. Success Metrics

*How will you know developers are better off? (adoption %, time-to-first-
service, DORA metrics of consuming teams, support tickets, NPS)*

-

## 9. What We Will NOT Do

*Explicit non-goals — the most underrated box. What stays with the
stream-aligned teams?*

-

---

### Debrief prompts (facilitator)

- Which box was hardest? (Usually 8 or 9 — discuss why.)
- Would developers *choose* this platform if it were optional? What is the
  contest-winning feature vs "do it yourself"?
- Where does today's capstone golden path fall short of your canvas?
