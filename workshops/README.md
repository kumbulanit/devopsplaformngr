# Hands-On Workshops (Module 10)

These three workshops are the **hands-on core of the course**, matching the
published outline exactly. Modules 1–9 are theory with small demos
(`instructor/theory-demos.md`); here you build for real, on your Ubuntu
24.04 VM, everything on **localhost**.

| # | Workshop | Outline topic | When | Time | Built from |
|---|----------|---------------|------|------|------------|
| 1 | [DevOps pipeline](workshop1-devops-pipeline.md) | *Setting up a basic DevOps pipeline* | Day 1, 15:30 | 105 min | Lab 04 (+ Lab 00 app) |
| 2 | [Build & deploy a microservice](workshop2-microservice.md) | *Building and deploying a microservice* | Day 2, 10:00 | 135 min | Labs 02 + 03 + 06 |
| 3 | [Implementing IaC](workshop3-iac.md) | *Implementing IaC for infrastructure* | Day 2, 14:30 | 60 min | Lab 05 (+ Ansible bonus) |

**Finale — the capstone** (Day 2, 16:30): `labs/lab09-capstone/` runs all
three workshops' material as one golden path and adds the platform-handover
document.

## Rules of the room

- **Done = the workshop's success criteria are checked**, not "I reached the
  bottom of the page."
- Hit an error? 1) troubleshooting table → 2) ask three neighbours → 3) ask
  the instructor.
- Finished early? Every workshop has stretch goals. They are real.

## Self-paced extras

Labs 01 (Git collaboration), 07 (full DevSecOps) and 08 (full observability)
are complete standalone labs you can run after the course — the theory
demos give you their essence in class.
